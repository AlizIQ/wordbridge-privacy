Privacy Policy for WordBridge

Last updated: August 23, 2026

WordBridge ("we", "our", "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: If you sign in, we collect your email address and/or Google account name to create your profile.
  • Quiz and usage data: We store your quiz scores, languages studied, and progress to power features like the leaderboard and your personal stats.
  • Purchase information: If you unlock premium content, purchase records are processed through Google Play Billing. We do not store your payment card details.

How We Use Your Information

  • To save your learning progress across sessions
  • To display your scores on the public leaderboard (your chosen display name/username is visible to other users; your email is not)
  • To unlock premium features after purchase

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Limited data may be shared with service providers strictly to operate the app (e.g. Google Play Billing for payments, Firebase for account and score storage).

Your Choices

  • You can use most features of the app without signing in.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below, or using the "Delete my account" option in Settings.

Children's Privacy

WordBridge is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
