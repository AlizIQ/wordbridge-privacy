Privacy Policy for WordBridge

Last updated: November 20, 2026

WordBridge ("we", "our", "the app"), developed by AlizIQ, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: If you choose to sign in with Google, we collect your Google account name, email address, profile photo URL, and Firebase User ID to create and manage your account.
  • Learning, quiz, and feature data: We store your learned words, Smart Review and mastery progress, daily streaks, quiz scores, languages studied, referral and promo redemption status, and 1v1 battle match scores to provide cloud sync, personal stats, and leaderboards.
  • User feedback and bug reports: If you submit feedback or a bug report in the app, we collect the message and category you provide so we can investigate issues and improve the app.
  • Purchase information: If you purchase a subscription or lifetime pass, transactions are processed through Google Play Billing. We store verified entitlement status and purchase tokens/order references to unlock and restore your premium access. We do not collect or store your payment card details.
  • Diagnostics and analytics: We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to collect app interaction events, crash logs, and basic device/OS diagnostics to fix bugs and improve performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To save and sync your vocabulary, Smart Review, and learning progress across sessions and devices.
  • To display your chosen display name, avatar, and scores on the public leaderboard and in 1v1 battles (your email address is never shown publicly).
  • To verify, unlock, and restore premium purchases, referrals, and promotional access.
  • To deliver optional daily study reminder notifications if you enable them.
  • To monitor app stability, diagnose crashes, and improve features.

3. Data Sharing and Service Providers

We do not sell your personal data. We share limited data only with trusted third-party infrastructure providers strictly necessary to operate the app:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Analytics, and Crashlytics) for sign-in, cloud storage, diagnostics, and analytics.
  • Google Play Billing for processing and verifying in-app purchases and subscriptions.

4. Data Security and Retention

All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. We retain your account and cloud progress data for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide the service.

5. Your Choices and Account Deletion

  • Optional sign-in: You can use core learning features of WordBridge without signing in.
  • Notifications: You can enable or disable reminder notifications at any time inside the app or in your Android device settings.

Delete your account inside WordBridge: Open the Profile screen in WordBridge and tap "Delete Account" to permanently delete your profile, leaderboard entry, cloud learning and Smart Review progress, and local app data.

Request deletion outside the app: If you have uninstalled WordBridge or need assistance, email us at alizainalaza345@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" from your registered email address. We will process account deletion requests within 7 days.

Note on retained records: Certain minimal purchase verification/audit records or anonymized aggregate statistics may be retained where required for billing verification, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

6. Children's Privacy

WordBridge is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data, please contact us so we can delete it promptly.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
